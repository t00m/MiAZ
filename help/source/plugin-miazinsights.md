# See insights into your documents

1. Enable the plugin in **Repository settings > Plugins**.
2. Right-click and open **Repository > Stats > Open insights**.

The **Browser** tab opens. If another page is showing, pick **Insights into
your documents** in its dropdown. The page shows:

- totals: documents, distinct senders, first and latest document, busiest year
  and month, longest streak;
- **Activity**: documents per year and per month; click a year to open it;
- leaders by sender, group, purpose and concept, with how their rank moved
  against the year before;
- a map of countries, shaded by how many documents came from each.

The page is rebuilt in the background whenever a document is added, renamed
or deleted.
