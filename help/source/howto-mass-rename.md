# Rename many documents at once

Mass renaming changes one field on every selected document.

1. Select two or more documents.
2. Press the rename button in the header bar, or right-click and open
   **Mass renaming**, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>M</kbd>.
3. Choose the field to change.
4. Pick the new value. A preview lists every old and new name.
5. Confirm.

## Dates {#dates}

When you change the date, **Detect date from each file** is on by default. Each
document then gets its own date, read from the file, instead of one date for
all. MiAZ reports how many dates it could read.

## Concept {#concept}

The Concept dialog transforms the existing text instead of replacing it: keep
or remove words, add a prefix, find and replace, or change the case.
