# Keep contact details

Every document names who sent it and who received it. MiAZContacts gives those
names a record: full name, addresses, phones, email addresses, web and social
profiles, bank accounts and a note. Records are kept as vCard.

## Add details {#add}

1. Enable the plugin in **Repository settings > Plugins**.
2. Switch to the **Contacts** view in the toolbar.
3. Pick a sender or recipient and press **Add details**, or create a **New
   contact**.
4. Fill in what you know and save.

A bank account needs both the holder and the IBAN.

## Find their documents {#documents}

From a contact, **Documents of** shows every document sent by or to them.

## Import and export {#vcard}

**Import** reads contacts from a vCard file; **Export** writes them to one, for
your address book or phone.
